Yen-Kang (Ted) Fu

I'm a second year math PhD student at The Ohio State University. My advisor is Michael Lipnowski.

My research interests center on number theory, particularly automorphic forms, representation theory, and related computational aspects.

I received my Master of Mathematics from the University of Waterloo under the supervision of Yu-Ru Liu. Prior to that, I received my Bachelor of Mathematics from the University of Waterloo.
